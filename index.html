KEEP LOOKING

The night begins with one bright shape.

At first, I only notice the moon.

It is bright, clear, and impossible to miss. For a moment, it feels like the whole night sky is just one object.

THEN I NOTICE A LIGHT

One small point changes where I look next.

One light becomes many.

The longer I look, the more details begin to appear. The darkness starts to feel less empty.

THE SKY GETS WIDER

Now there is no single place to focus.

The night is bigger than I first thought.

What looked simple at the beginning starts to feel layered. More stars appear, and the space between them begins to matter too.

LOOK CLOSER

Some things only appear when I stay with them.

Looking longer changes the view.

Nothing in the sky had to move for my attention to change. I just had to keep looking.

Keep looking.

The view can change even when the world stays still.
